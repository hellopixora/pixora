Answers for curious patients

Click "Book Appointment" from any page. Our 5-step booking flow lets you choose a physician, pick a date, select a time, choose in-person or telehealth, and confirm — all in under 3 minutes. No phone calls, no hold music.

Absolutely. Pixora Doc 1 is fully HIPAA compliant and SOC 2 Type II certified. Your records are encrypted at rest and in transit using 256-bit AES. We never sell or share your data with advertisers, employers, or insurers without your explicit written consent.

We accept most major PPO and HMO plans, including Aetna, BlueCross BlueShield, Cigna, United Healthcare, and Humana. Telehealth visits are often covered at the same rate as in-person visits under most plans. If you're uninsured, our self-pay rates start at $89 per visit.

Our Symptom Checker is a triage tool — not a diagnostic engine. It helps match your symptoms to the most relevant specialist so you're not navigating medical directories alone. It is not a substitute for a physician evaluation, and your data is never saved or stored after you leave the page.

Yes. We support FHIR-compliant record imports, which means your records from Epic, Cerner, Athena, and most major EHR systems can be transferred automatically. You can also upload documents manually. Our care team assists with transfers at no extra charge.

For life-threatening emergencies, always call 911 or go to your nearest ER. For urgent (non-emergency) issues, Pixora Doc 1 offers same-day telehealth slots with on-call physicians, typically available within 2 hours. You can also message your care team 24/7 through SecureChat.
