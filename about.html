Our story

We obsess over coffee so you don't have to.

From sourcing to the cup — every decision is deliberate.

Pixora Cafe 1 was born from a single conviction: that specialty coffee doesn't have to feel exclusive. It should be immediate, atmospheric, and real — something you return to not just for the caffeine, but for what it makes you feel.

We opened our doors in 2024 with a tight menu, a relentless commitment to single-origin beans, and an obsession with cold foam that borders on the unreasonable. Our sourcing relationships span Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — farms we've visited, farmers we know by name.

Every drink on our menu was developed through dozens of iterations. Nothing ships until it's exactly right. We make cold foam fresh every morning, pull espresso to exacting standards, and treat each cup as the beginning of someone's ritual — not just a transaction.

Pixora Cafe 1 is built around a simple idea: a great cup of coffee is an act of care. We hope you feel it every time.
